Our Story

Built from
every late night.

Harmony Events & Productions was founded by students from IIT Madras who lived through the chaos of campus event planning — vendor no-shows, opaque pricing, last-minute crises. We built the company we wished had existed.

Today we bring the same rigour we applied to our coursework to every event — whether it's a cultural festival for 5,000 or a corporate conclave for 50. Every event is a project. Every project is handled.
